The thread through all of it.

I like taking the messy thing, a year of spreadsheets, 32 hours of audio, a phone store's inventory chaos, and turning it into a system people actually use. Not a demo. The version that is still running six months later.

Right now that means production code at a ride-hailing startup, IT at a manufacturing plant, and AI pipelines with verification built in. I study computer science at UofL's Speed School with a minor in Arabic, which is why the school platform I built ships full RTL and why this site keeps a little Arabic in its corners.

This site is hand-coded: no framework, no build step. View source; that's the point.
